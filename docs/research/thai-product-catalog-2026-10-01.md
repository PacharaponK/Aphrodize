## Price and formula limitations

The clean English CeraVe Cream product page showed 179 THB while the Thai-language page showed 210 THB. Oil Control's opened Thai page showed 655 THB while categories/indexed snippets showed 499 THB. Other CeraVe product snippets also differed from opened clean product URLs. The candidate always records the exact product-page URL supporting its selected price; it is not a price guarantee, minimum-across-market claim, or shopping-cart confirmation. Recheck checkout prices before purchase.

**Excluded Daily Moisturising Lotion 236 ml:** Thai manufacturer lists methylparaben/propylparaben and hydrolyzed hyaluronic acid while the Thai retailer lists phenoxyethanol and sodium hyaluronate. Both are real Thai sources, but the exact retail formula is inconsistent. Do not merge them or transplant the overseas formula. Actual packaging or a corrected manufacturer SKU page is needed before publishing this variant. [Thai manufacturer](https://www.cerave.co.th/skincare/daily-moisturising-lotion), [Watsons 236 ml](https://www.watsons.co.th/th/cerave-%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%8B%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%A7%E0%B8%B5-%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%A2%E0%B8%8B%E0%B9%8C%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%88%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%A3%E0%B9%8C%E0%B9%84%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%8B%E0%B8%B4%E0%B9%88%E0%B8%87-%E0%B9%82%E0%B8%A5%E0%B8%8A%E0%B8%B1%E0%B9%88%E0%B8%99-236-%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%A5./p/BP_308851).

Manufacturer descriptions are label-backed claims, not independent efficacy validation or allergy clearance. Existing named-product withholding for reported allergy remains appropriate. Treatments are limited to adult users and released, eligible image regions. Face products must not be routed to eye contour merely because wrinkles were detected there. All consumers should check the physical packaging for current ingredients; this research did not inspect packaging or guarantee clinical suitability.

## Follow-up: combination-skin fragrance-free moisturizer gap

No ninth verified candidate was added. **Bioderma Sensibio Defensive, 40 ml** has product-specific Thai manufacturer claims for lightweight, fast-absorbing, fragrance-free, non-comedogenic care and a full INCI list matching the Thai Watsons list. The opened Watsons page displayed **940 THB** (a search snippet showed 752 THB, which was not used). However, the Thai manufacturer classifies this product as sensitive/reactive skin, and its moisturizer article mentions dry skin; neither source explicitly establishes combination or all skin types. Do not translate sensitive-skin suitability into an all-skin-types target. [Thai manufacturer product and full INCI](https://www.bioderma.co.th/our-products/sensibio/defensive), [manufacturer skin-type article](https://www.bioderma.co.th/your-skin/dry-skin/Moisturizerforskins), [opened Thai Watsons 40 ml price and INCI](https://www.watsons.co.th/th/bioderma-ไบโอเดอร์ม่า-เซ็นซิบิโอ-ดีเฟนซีฟ-40-มล./p/BP_297381).

**Cetaphil Moisturising Lotion** has fragrance-free and lightweight claims on its Thai manufacturer site, but its explicit target is dry-to-normal skin, which does not fill the combination-skin gap. [Thai manufacturer exact product](https://www.cetaphil.co.th/en/moisturizers/cetaphil-moisturising-lotion/3499320013238TH.html).

**CeraVe Oil Control** remains without a fragrance-free tag because its specific Thai product page does not state that claim. No individual product should inherit those broad assertions without formula-specific evidence. [Specific Oil Control page](https://www.cerave.co.th/skincare/oil-control-moisturising-gel-cream), [brand FAQ considered and excluded](https://www.cerave.co.th/faq).
